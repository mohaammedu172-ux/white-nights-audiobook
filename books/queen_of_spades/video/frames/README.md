# Frames

23 illustrations, AI-generated (2026-10-25) from prompts written for this book, in the style of Paul Hardy's
1891 illustrations for The Strand Magazine. Mohammed made them in an image AI as grids; each panel was cut
out, upscaled 3x with OpenCV EDSR, then turned into a 1920x1080 ink-on-paper frame by ../prepare_frames.py.
Scene timing: ../scenes.json. s04 is used twice (her toilette at 3:43 and again at 8:33, when she comes home).
