# Voice reference credits

`clartts_male_ref.wav` (18.9 s, 24 kHz) is joined from two clips of the **ClArTTS** corpus
(Classical Arabic Text-to-Speech, Kulkarni et al., 2023), files `ch_02_arabic_tts_dataset_417` and `ch_09_arabic_tts_dataset_352`.
ClArTTS is released under **CC BY 4.0**. The recordings come from a LibriVox audiobook read by **Youssef Safiljil**.
The transcript of the reference clip is in `clartts_male_ref.txt`.

Please credit "Voice reference: ClArTTS corpus (CC BY 4.0), reader Youssef Safiljil (LibriVox)" in video descriptions.

Models used to make the audio:
- Chatterbox Multilingual by Resemble AI, ONNX export `onnx-community/chatterbox-multilingual-ONNX` (MIT)
- Judges: `NAMAA-Space/Cohere-Speech-Tashkeel-2B` (Apache-2.0), `rufaelfekadu/diactc-wav2vec2-clartts` (MIT)
